EDITORIAL PROCESS

CHECK WHAT CHANGED.
CHECK WHAT DIDN'T.

A disciplined 5-step process diagram ensuring intent, context and meaning remain intact.

01 / READ

Understand Intent

Grasp the intended meaning, audience and emotional tone before touching a single character.

02 / CHECK

Find Flaws

Locate factual errors, linguistic slips, structural gaps or hallucinated AI phrasing.

03 / REFINE

Remove Friction

Improve clarity, rhythm and flow without unnecessarily rewriting the author's voice.

04 / COMPARE

Verify Source

Cross-check output against original prompt instructions to verify strict compliance.

05 / VERIFY

User Perspective

Read the result from the end user's perspective to guarantee clean, reliable reception.
